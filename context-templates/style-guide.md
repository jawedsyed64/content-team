## Sentences and paragraphs

- Paragraphs: 2–4 sentences on the web.
- Prefer active voice.

## Headings

## Formatting
- Use bullet lists for 3+ parallel items.
- Bold sparingly for key terms only.
- Output format for drafts: Markdown.

## Citations and references
- Every statistic, quotation and factual claim that is not common knowledge
  must have a source.
- Cite inline as a link on the claim, and list sources at the end under
  "Sources".
- Prefer primary sources (official statistics, original research, company
  filings) over secondary reporting.
